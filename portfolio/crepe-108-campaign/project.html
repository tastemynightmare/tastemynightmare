EXPLORE THE CAMPAIGN

The idea behind it

Crêpe began as a F.I.R.S.T. Institute menu and logo project. I expanded that original work into a coordinated campaign, preserving the gold wordmark while introducing a refreshed palette, blue wrap-and-spoon illustrations, and six kawaii flavor characters. The identity carries across the logo, menu, packaging, and collectible stickers.
