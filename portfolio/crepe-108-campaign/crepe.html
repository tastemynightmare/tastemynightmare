The process / in progress

From the very first menu
to a kawaii dessert world.

Crêpe began as a F.I.R.S.T. Institute class project, and now expands into a full brand campaign! I will present to you an updated logo, dessert menu, mockups, and a super Kawaii sticker collection! This campaign develops that existing work into four coordinated deliverables rather than starting with an unrelated brand. Come on in.. this Nightmare is topped with whipped cream!

  1. 01 / Revisit the original

    The original Crêpe logo, menu and style guide provide the foundation. The gold lettering, chocolate drips, folded crêpe, blue wrap and curved blue spoon are the visual elements carried into this refresh.

  2. 02 / Define the direction

    “Pick your sweet mood” connects flavor to personality. The updated direction adds kawaii expressions and flavor-specific toppings while preserving the original cone-and-spoon concept.

  3. 03 / Build the logo in Illustrator

    Next: keep the original gold Crêpe wordmark and folded-crêpe icon, then incorporate the blue wrap and blue spoon. Create a full-color primary lockup, a simplified icon and approved monochrome versions. Save development screenshots for the final process post.

  4. 04 / Redesign the menu

    Next: create a menu with Fredoka section headings and flavor names, Nunito descriptions and prices, consistent spacing, and aligned price columns. Clarify which items are served in a cone or bowl before finalizing the category names.

  5. 05 / Formalize and extend

    Next: create the updated style guide with accurate color values, typography, logo rules and illustration examples. Develop six cone-and-spoon flavor characters into a coordinated sticker collection.

  6. 06 / Present the final work

    Next: export each of the four deliverables as a flat image and present it in an application mockup. Add those images to the slots below, replace planning notes with completed steps, and document the design decisions with real progress images.

Project status: The original logo and menu are existing school work. Here I completely revamped the brand with a fresh take! The six-character sticker sheet is a fun kawaii exploration.

Original logo lettering

Keep the original Crêpe script as vector artwork. Fredoka and Nunito support the logo; they do not replace its lettering. Add the blue wrap and spoon to the updated illustration in Illustrator.

Logo use

Keep proportions intact and use the original “C” height as the clear-space guide. Use approved full-color or monochrome versions. Place the logo on quiet backgrounds and avoid stretching, recoloring or adding effects.

Illustration + tone

Repeat the folded crêpe, blue overlapping wrap, curved spoon, chocolate outlines and little face. Differentiate flavors through recognizable toppings and expressions. Keep the language warm, short and playful.

The font pairing

A little sweet. A little cheeky.

Golden crêpes, dreamy toppings, and tiny flavor friends. Pick a treat that matches your mood.

Fredoka gives headings a rounded, playful voice. Nunito carries the details with a softer, quieter rhythm. The original script remains the brand signature.

What this project showcases

This campaign is designed to showcase brand strategy, logo refinement, vector illustration, menu hierarchy, typography, mockup deliverables, and collectible sticker designs.

The creative direction takes the original concept and adds a little more child-like flair that helps keep the identity rooted in the original, but updates and integrates a new wrap-and-spoon design.
