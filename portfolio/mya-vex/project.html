The idea behind it

I created Mya Vex to build my web design and development portfolio and connect with the tattoo industry. Tattoo artists bring so much creativity and personality to their work, and I wanted to show how their websites could do the same. This concept gave me room to explore bold color, expressive layouts, and a strong visual identity beyond the constraints of a corporate brief. The gallery and inquiry demo bring that creative direction together with a clear next step for potential clients.
