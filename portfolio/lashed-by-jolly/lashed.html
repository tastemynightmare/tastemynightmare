The idea behind it

I created Lashed by Jolly to build my web design and development portfolio and connect with local beauty businesses. I wanted potential clients to experience my work before I even had to introduce myself. The concept pairs a feminine, sophisticated visual style with a lash gallery, clear pricing, and an interactive booking demo, showing how a website can bring a beauty brand to life.
