06 // PROJECT PROTOCOL

BEFORE WE RENDER.

BOOKING + PAYMENT

Projects of $150 or more: 50% to book, 50% before public launch or final file delivery. Smaller projects are paid in full before work begins.

REVISIONS + RUSH WORK

Two consolidated rounds within the approved direction. Extra revisions are $60/hour, with a one-hour minimum agreed in advance. Rush delivery adds 25% to affected work when available.

CONTENT + TIMELINE

Production starts after the booking payment, final content, and required access arrive. Waiting on feedback or provider setup shifts delivery. Website packages cover one brand and one language.

OWN YOUR ACCOUNTS

Keep domains, subscriptions, and hosting in your account and pay providers directly. Your proposal confirms the exact scope, deliverables, and schedule before work starts.
