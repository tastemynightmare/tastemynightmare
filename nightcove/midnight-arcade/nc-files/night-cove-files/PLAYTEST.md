# First blind play-test

Please test without reading the creator solution or source-code answers. Start with a new save (Notebook → Start this case again), and note your starting time.

1. Can you explain who died, who disappeared, and what you are trying to establish after reading the case file?
2. Inspect the first packet in any order. Can you tell original records from 2026 supplements?
3. Pin sources and make the first finding. Deliberately try one incorrect explanation. Is the response useful and non-punishing?
4. Continue without a hint until you feel stuck. Record the exact source or deduction. Then try hint levels one at a time.
5. Stop midway, close the tab, and return. Check notes, pins, and findings.
6. Complete the chronology and final reconstruction. Does the evidence persuade you independently of the answer choices?
7. Finish the family ending and restored recording. Record your finish time and hints used.

Test on a phone as well as a desktop. At 200% browser zoom, check that text reflows and controls remain reachable. Use only keyboard Tab/Shift+Tab/Enter/Space, with Escape to close. Check that focus returns to the case cabinet. Turn on larger text. Read the map's textual equivalent. If possible, try VoiceOver or NVDA and a second browser.
